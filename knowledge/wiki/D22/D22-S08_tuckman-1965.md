## 2. 要旨（読んだ内容に基づく）

Tuckman は 1965 年時点で存在した小集団発達研究 50 篇（therapy group 26、T-group 11、natural/laboratory group 13）を設定別にレビューし、「group structure（集団構造）」と「task activity（課題活動）」という 2 つの realm において、4 段階の発達系列が横断的に同定できることを論じる。各段階は Stage 1: Testing and Dependence / Orientation、Stage 2: Intragroup Conflict / Emotional Response to Task Demands、Stage 3: Development of Group Cohesion / Open Exchange of Relevant Interpretations、Stage 4: Functional Role-Relatedness / Emergence of Solutions と命名される。Tuckman 自身はこの 4 段階を「仮説的整理 (a conceptual statement)」と位置付けており、後年有名になる "forming–storming–norming–performing" のリズミック命名は 1977 年の改訂版（Tuckman & Jensen）で登場する。

## 3. 主要主張（原文引用付き）

**注記**: これは survey/review 論文であるため、以下の「主張」は著者オリジナルの理論というより、50 篇の研究群から帰納された整理提案である。

### 主張 1: 集団発達は「group structure」と「task activity」の 2 realms に分けられ、それぞれ 4 段階を経る

> "50 articles dealing with stages of group development over time are separated by group setting, as follows: therapy-group studies, T-group studies, and natural- and laboratory-group studies. The stages identified in these articles are separated into those descriptive of social or interpersonal group activities and those descriptive of group-task activities. Finally, 4 general stages of development are proposed, and the review consists of fitting the stages identified in the literature to those proposed." (p.384, Abstract)

著者は二重構造（構造/課題 × 4 段階）を軸に、多様な研究文献を整理可能であることを示す。

### 主張 2: Stage 1 は「Testing and Dependence / Orientation」であり、リーダーや課題への依存・探索によって特徴づけられる

> "In the realm of group structure the first hypothesized stage of the model is labeled as *testing and dependence*. The term 'testing' refers to an attempt by group members to discover what interpersonal behaviors are acceptable in the group, based on the reactions of the therapist or trainer ... and on the reactions of the other group members." (p.386)

> "The first stage of task-activity development is labeled as *orientation to the task*, in which group members attempt to identify the task in terms of its relevant parameters and the manner in which the group experience will be used to accomplish the task. The group must decide upon the type of information they will need in dealing with the task and how this information is to be obtained." (p.386)

テストと依存は対人的側面、オリエンテーションは課題側面の初期行動。両者は同時発生するが分析的には区別される。

### 主張 3: Stage 2 は「Intragroup Conflict / Emotional Response to Task Demands」であり、抵抗と極化が特徴

> "The second phase in the development of group structure is identified as the second stage of task-activity development. Group members become hostile toward one another and toward a therapist or trainer as a means of expressing their individuality and resisting the formation of group structure. Interaction is uneven and 'infighting' is common." (p.386)

> "Emotional response to task demands is identified as the second stage of task-activity development. Group members react emotionally to the task as a form of resistance to the demands of the task on the individual." (p.386)

構造面の対立と課題面の情動的抵抗は「集団に吸収されることへの抵抗」という共通力学を持つ。

### 主張 4: Stage 3 は「Development of Group Cohesion / Open Exchange of Relevant Interpretations」

> "The third group structure phase is labeled as the *development of group cohesion*. Group members accept the group and accept the idiosyncracies of fellow members. The group becomes an entity by virtue of its acceptance by the members, their desire to maintain and perpetuate it, and the establishment of new group-generated norms to insure the group's existence. Harmony is of maximum importance, and task conflicts are avoided to insure harmony." (p.386)

> "The third stage of task activity development is labeled as the *open exchange of relevant interpretations*. In the therapy- and training-group context, this takes the form of discussing oneself and other group members, since self and other personal characteristics are the basic task inputs. In the laboratory-task context, exchanged interpretations take the form of opinions." (p.387)

Stage 3 は「結束確立と規範生成」および「オープンな情報交換」の両面を持つ。

### 主張 5: Stage 4 は「Functional Role-Relatedness / Emergence of Solutions」であり、構造が課題達成の道具となる

> "The fourth and final developmental phase of group structure is labeled as *functional role-relatedness*. The group, which was established during the preceding phase, can now become a problem-solving instrument. It does this by directing itself to members as objects, since the subjective relationship between members has already been established. Members can now adopt and play roles that will enhance the task activities of the group, since they have learned to relate to one another as social entities in the preceding stage. Role structure is not an issue per se at this point, because the group becomes a 'sounding board' off which the task is 'played'." (p.387)

> "In task-activity development, the fourth and final stage is identified as the *emergence of solutions*. It is here that we observe constructive attempts at successful task completion." (p.387)

Stage 4 では集団構造が「他者を対象化し役割を柔軟に演じる透明な道具」となり、課題面では解決策が出現する。

### 主張 6: この 4 段階モデルは心理療法・訓練集団・自然/実験集団の 3 設定で並列確認される

> "Of the 26 studies of therapy groups which were reviewed, 18 identified a beginning stage as either testing or dependence or both ..." (p.387)

> "Nine of the 11 training-group studies reviewed that deal with the development of group structure identify an initial stage by testing and dependence, with the emphasis on the dependent aspect of this stage." (p.391)

設定ごとに用語は異なるが、発達系列の基本形は 3 設定で共通すると著者は論じる。

## 4. 方法論

- **二次研究（ナラティブ・レビュー）**: 50 篇の既存研究を設定別（therapy / T-group / natural+lab）・realm 別（structure / task）に分類し、各研究が同定する段階を 4 段階テンプレートにマッピング
- **用語の異同の吸収**: 各研究が用いる語（acclimatization, attachment, hesitant participation, structuralization, absolutistic dependency など）を Stage 1 の下位として束ねる帰納的整理
- **実証データなし**: 著者自身による新規実験や観察はなく、既存文献の「conceptual vehicle」として 4 段階を提案（著者自身が "not suggested for primary use as an organizational vehicle, although it serves that function here" と明言, p.386）
- **Prestage の提示**: 一部の薬物依存・非行集団研究では Stage 1 以前の「resistance/silence/hostility」期が存在することを "Prestage 1" として注記（p.388）

## 5. 5段階との対応候補

| 段階 | 対応候補 | 強度 | 原文引用 |
|------|---------|------|---------|
| 1 場 (Field) | 集団設定（therapy/training/lab）そのものが発達の「場」として区別される | 弱 | "Classification according to setting allows for the clustering of studies based on their similarity of features, for example, group size, group problem area, group composition, duration of 'group life', etc." (p.384) |
| 2 波 (Wave) | Stage 2 の情動的抵抗・「unevenness と infighting」は集合的揺動として波に対応 | 弱 | "Interaction is uneven and 'infighting' is common. There are characteristic key issues that polarize the group and boil down to the conflict about their progression into the 'unknown' of interpersonal relations or regression to the security of earlier dependence." (p.386) |
| 3 縁 (Relation) | Stage 1 のリーダー・他メンバーとの境界探索と Stage 3 の結束形成は「縁」の設定と強化 | 強 | "The term 'testing' refers to an attempt by group members to discover what interpersonal behaviors are acceptable in the group" (p.386); "Group members accept the group and accept the idiosyncracies of fellow members. The group becomes an entity by virtue of its acceptance by the members" (p.386) |
| 4 渦 (Vortex) | Stage 4 の「functional role-relatedness」は役割の流動的配分による自己駆動的な課題達成構造 | 強 | "Role structure is not an issue per se at this point, because the group becomes a 'sounding board' off which the task is 'played'" (p.387) |
| 5 束 (Bundle) | 4 段階を通じて「group-as-entity」が個々の役割を統合する最終形態 | 弱 | "The group, which was established during the preceding phase, can now become a problem-solving instrument" (p.387) — 束化された集団の道具化 |

**判定基準の適用**:
- **Stage 3/4 強**: Tuckman の枠組みの中核は「関係の形成 (testing→cohesion)」と「役割の機能化 (functional role-relatedness)」であり、Relation/Vortex に強対応
- **Stage 2 弱**: 情動的抵抗段階は構造的に「波」に類比可能だが、著者の意図は「課題への情動反応」であり物理的波動の比喩とは距離がある
- **Stage 1/5 弱**: 設定分類や集団-as-entity は副次的な読みであり、著者の焦点ではない

**manifest ヒントからの独立性**: manifest ヒントには「Stage 1-4: 集団発達 4 段階」とあった。原典の 4 段階（testing→conflict→cohesion→role-relatedness）と表面的には一致するが、5段階モデル（場/波/縁/渦/束）と Tuckman 4 段階は意味論が異なる（発達時系列 vs. 構造原理）。Tuckman は「創造の生成位相」ではなく「集団成熟の時間系列」を論じており、直接対応させると牽強付会になる。Stage 3/4 強、他は弱という原典独立判定。

## 6. 限界・留意事項

- **論文種別**: これは review 論文であり、Tuckman 独自の実証データや新しい理論構築ではない。4 段階は「文献整理のための conceptual vehicle」として提案されており、著者自身もその暫定性を明示する (p.386)
- **「forming/storming/norming/performing」の語は 1965 年原典にない**: 後年の Tuckman & Jensen (1977) — D22-S09 — で初めて韻を踏んだ名称が与えられる。本論文の命名は "Testing and Dependence"、"Intragroup Conflict"、"Development of Group Cohesion"、"Functional Role-Relatedness"
- **創造プロセスとの類比は原典に存在しない**: 著者の問題設定は小集団の社会心理学的発達であり、「創造の位相遷移」ではない。5段階対応は構造類似の判定にすぎず、著者の意図ではない
- **レビュー対象の偏り**: 50 篇中 26 篇が therapy group であり、集団の目的が「治癒」である設定に重みが置かれている。創造・問題解決を主目的とする集団への一般化には留保が必要
