## 1. 書誌情報

- **著者**: Philippe Rochat (Department of Psychology, Emory University)
- **タイトル**: Five levels of self-awareness as they unfold early in life
- **出典**: *Consciousness and Cognition* 12 (2003), 717-731

## 2. 要旨（読んだ内容に基づく）

Rochat は乳幼児の自己意識発達を「Level 0 (Confusion) から Level 5 (Meta self-awareness)」の 6 階梯モデルで定式化する（Level 0 を「自己意識の不在」とし、Level 1-5 が自己意識の段階）。鏡像反応研究 (mirror-mark task) を中心的な行動指標としつつ、出生直後の自己/非自己触覚弁別 (rooting response)、2 ヶ月時点の contemplative stance、second year の "Me" の誕生、そして 4-5 歳前後の self-conscious emotions の出現を時系列で対応付ける。著者は最終的に、成人の自己意識を「これら基底レベル間の動的フラックス (dynamic flux)」として捉える視座を提示する。

## 3. 主要主張（原文引用付き）

### 主張 1: 自己意識は 6 階梯（Level 0 + Level 1-5）として時系列展開する

> "Based on some recent empirical evidence, 5 levels of self-awareness are presented and discussed as they chronologically unfold from the moment of birth to approximately 4–5 years of age. A natural history of children's developing self-awareness is proposed as well as a model of adult self-awareness that is informed by the dynamic of early development." (p.717, Abstract)

著者は 5 levels を Level 0 (Confusion), Level 1 (Differentiation), Level 2 (Situation), Level 3 (Identification), Level 4 (Permanence), Level 5 (Meta self-awareness) と命名する。Level 0 は self-awareness の不在を示すゼロ点であり、Level 1-5 が段階的進展を表す。Levels 0-2 は implicit、Levels 3-5 は explicit と区別される。

### 主張 2: Level 0 (Confusion) — 鏡像と環境の混同

> "This is the degree zero of self-awareness, level 0 at which the individual is oblivious of any mirror reflection, thus oblivious of the mirror itself. The specular image is confounded with the reality of the environment it reflects. It is perceived as a mere extension of the world, not a reflection of it." (p.719)

鳥が窓に衝突すること、犬猫やサルが鏡像に対して攻撃姿勢を続けることが例示される。成人でもまれに自分の鏡像に「他人がいる」と一瞬感じる現象として残る (p.719)。

### 主張 3: Level 1 (Differentiation) — 鏡像と環境の知覚的弁別

> "At this level, there is a sense that what is perceived in the mirror is different from what is perceived in the surrounding environment. More specifically, when perceiving the own specular image, the individual picks up the fact that there is something unique about the experience, namely that there is a perfect contingency between seen and felt movements. ... This is a first level of self-world differentiation: a differentiated self is expressed." (p.720)

「視覚-運動の完全な随伴性」を手がかりに、自己が世界から分化される最初の段階。出生直後から見られる self/non-self touch 弁別（rooting response）が経験的根拠 (pp.722-723)。

### 主張 4: Level 2 (Situation) — 鏡面と身体の空間的位置関係

> "Beyond the differentiation of the uniqueness of self-produced movements seen on the surface of the mirror, the individual now is capable of systematically exploring the intermodal link between seen movements on the mirror surface and what is perceived of the own body proprioceptively. ... a situated self is expressed." (pp.720-721)

contemplative stance が 2 ヶ月以降に出現。Meltzoff & Moore の模倣実験（舌の左右弁別模倣）、proto-conversation、4-6 ヶ月の reaching における姿勢適応などが経験的根拠 (pp.723-725)。

### 主張 5: Level 3 (Identification) — 鏡像が「私」であるという認識

> "At this level, the individual manifests recognition, the fact that what is in the mirror is 'Me,' not another individual staring and shadowing the self. ... This level is expressed when children refer explicitly to the self while exploring their own specular image. ... an identified self is expressed." (pp.721-722)

mirror-mark test での自己接触行動が指標。Lewis & Brooks-Gunn (1979), Gallup (1982), Povinelli (1993) を引用。

### 主張 6: Level 4 (Permanence) — 時間と外見の変化を超えた自己同一性

> "The self is identified beyond the here and now of mirror experience. It can be identified in pictures and movies taken in the past, where the self might be significantly younger, at a different location and dressed in different cloths. ... A permanent self is expressed: an entity that is represented as invariant over time and appearance changes." (p.722)

過去の写真や動画における自己の同定能力。

### 主張 7: Level 5 (Meta Self-awareness) — 他者の心の中の自己

> "The self is now recognized not only from a first person perspective, but also from a third person's. Individuals are not only aware of what they are but how they are in the mind of others: How they present themselves to the public eye (Goffman, 1959). The public outlook on the self is simulated for further evaluation of how one is perceived and valued by others. ... A self-conscious self is expressed: an entity that is simulated and projected in the mind of others." (p.722)

embarrassment, shame, pride などの self-conscious emotions が指標。Rimbaud "Je est quelqu'un d'autre" や Goffman の dramaturgical 視点と関連付けられる。

### 主張 8: 出生直後から self-world differentiation が存在する

> "Recent empirical findings suggest that infants do not come to the world with the exclusive expression of self-obliviousness corresponding to Level 0, the degree zero of self-awareness as described above. It appears that immediately after birth, infants are capable of demonstrating already a sense of their own body as a differentiated entity ... Contrary to the assumption of many classic theories of child development, infants are not born in a state of fusion or confusion with environment (the 'blooming, buzzing, confusion' proposed by William James, 1890)." (pp.722-723)

新生児の rooting response の self/non-self 弁別研究 (Rochat & Hespos, 1997) が根拠。James 流の融合的乳児観を実証的に否定する。

## 4. 方法論

- **発達心理学的レビュー＋自身の研究の総合**: 鏡像反応・新生児の rooting response・乳児の reaching と postural control・proto-conversation など、複数の経験パラダイムから 5 階梯モデルを再構築
- **経験的指標の階層化**: Level 0-5 はそれぞれ観察可能な行動指標と対応付けられる: 鏡への無関心 (L0), 視-運動随伴性の認識 (L1), contemplative stance / 模倣 (L2), mirror-mark test 通過 (L3), 過去の自己写真同定 (L4), 鏡前 embarrassment (L5)
- **比較心理学の参照**: 動物実験（Gallup の chimp mirror test, Povinelli）と異文化比較（Carpenter による Papua New Guinea Biami 部族の鏡像初遭遇反応, p.719）を引用
- **forward engineering の比喩**: 発達観察を「エッフェル塔の建設写真集」に喩える (p.717)。最終形を分解するのではなく構築過程を観察する

## 5. 5段階との対応候補

| 段階 | 対応候補 | 強度 | 原文引用 |
|------|---------|------|---------|
| 1 場 (Field) | Level 0 Confusion: 自己と環境が未分化な「場」 | 強 | "The specular image is confounded with the reality of the environment it reflects. It is perceived as a mere extension of the world, not a reflection of it." (p.719) |
| 2 波 (Wave) | Level 1 Differentiation: 視-運動随伴性という波動的時間構造の検出 | 弱 | "the perfect contingency between seen and felt movements" (p.720) — 構造的類比だが「波」とは異なる |
| 3 縁 (Relation) | Level 2 Situation: 鏡面と身体の空間関係の探索 / Level 3 Identification: 自他の境界 | 強 | "individuals now go beyond the awareness of matched surface characteristics ... an image that is out there, projecting back at them what they feel from within" (pp.720-721) |
| 4 渦 (Vortex) | Level 4 Permanence: 時間軸を貫いて自己が同一性を保つ自律的構造 | 弱 | "an entity that is represented as invariant over time and appearance changes" (p.722) |
| 5 束 (Bundle) | Level 5 Meta self-awareness: 自己と他者からの視線が束ねられた reflexive 構造 | 強 | "The self is now recognized not only from a first person perspective, but also from a third person's. Individuals are not only aware of what they are but how they are in the mind of others" (p.722) |

**判定基準の適用**:
- **Stage 1/3/5 強**: Level 0 (未分化な場), Level 2-3 (空間的・対人的縁の確立), Level 5 (自己と他者視点の束化) はそれぞれ 5段階モデルの場/縁/束に直接対応する
- **Stage 2/4 弱**: Wave と Vortex は構造的類比はあるが、Rochat の文脈は「発達時系列」であり「位相遷移」ではない

**manifest ヒントからの独立性**: manifest ヒントには「Stage 1-5: 5 levels of self-awareness そのもの」とあった。表面的な「5 levels」の一致から「全段階強対応」と読みたくなるが、原典の 5 levels は (a) 発達時系列であり構造原理ではない、(b) Level 0 は不在を示すゼロ点で他のレベルと並列ではない、という違いがある。Maslow (D14-S08) と同様、「5 段階」という表面的一致に引きずられない判定が必要。Stage 1/3/5 強、2/4 弱という結論は表面ヒントを修正する方向。

## 6. 限界・留意事項

- **論文種別**: 発達心理学のレビュー＋著者自身の研究のシンセシス論文。新規実験データは限定的で、既存パラダイムの整理が中心
- **「creative process」との直接的関連は皆無**: Rochat の論題は乳幼児の自己意識発達であり、「創造の生成」ではない。5段階対応は構造類似の判定にすぎない
- **mirror-mark test の生態学的妥当性への留保**: 著者自身が "Mirrors have been criticized for lacking ecological validity (Loveland, 1986) and mirror self-recognition for not being a thorough index of self-awareness (Povinelli, 1995, 2001)" と注記 (p.719)。鏡像課題以外の自己意識指標との交差検証が課題
- **Level の連続性 vs. 離散性**: 6 階梯は離散的に提示されるが、本文では Level 1-2 や Level 4-5 の境界が連続的である旨が示唆される。離散段階モデルとしての厳密さには留保が必要
- **Adult self-awareness を「dynamic flux between levels」とする視座**: 抄録でこの主張があるが、本文ではこの adult model の詳細展開は §3 以降に置かれる可能性がある（未読部分）
