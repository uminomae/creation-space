## 2. 要旨

1967 書籍 *Biological Foundations of Language* は言語の生物学的基盤を体系化したマイルストーン著作で、(i) 言語発達の **maturational predictability**（運動発達との並走）、(ii) **critical period**（思春期早期までの言語獲得感受期）、(iii) 大脳半球の **lateralization** 発達、(iv) **species-specific** 認知統合、(v) **language universals**（関係性の関係性）を理論化した。1969 Science 要約論文は同書の中心命題を 9 頁に凝縮し、以下の 6 つの言語特性を冒頭で宣言する: 全文化普遍性、年齢相関的発症、単一獲得戦略、形式的操作特性の同一性、歴史的不変性、特定脳病変による選択的損失。著者は **「言語は脳の operating principles の表現であり、artifact（道具）ではない」** という立場を堅持し、Skinner 学派の刺激-反応学習説、Chomsky 学派の生成文法理論の双方に対して **発達生物学（developmental biology）** の枠組みから接近する。D17（言語学）視点では、本論文は Bickerton 1981（D17-S07）、Hartshorne et al. 2018（D17-S10）、Beckner et al. 2017（D17-S11）といった臨界期・成熟・自己組織化系の言語発達研究の理論的祖型として位置付けられる。

## 3. 主要主張（原文引用付き）

### 主張 1: 言語の 6 特性が生物学的基盤の存在を示唆する

> "(i) It is a form of behavior present in all cultures of the world. (ii) In all cultures its onset is age correlated. (iii) There is only one acquisition strategy—it is the same for all babies everywhere in the world. (iv) It is based intrinsically upon the same formal operating characteristics whatever its outward form (1). (v) Throughout man's recorded history these operating characteristics have been constant. (vi) It is a form of behavior that may be impaired specifically by circumscribed brain lesions which may leave other mental and motor skills relatively unaffected. Any form of human behavior that has all of these six characteristics may likewise be assumed to have a rather specific biological foundation." (p.635)

これは方法論的宣言。普遍性 + 年齢相関 + 単一戦略 + 形式同一性 + 歴史不変性 + 選択的脳病変が揃えば、その行動は **specific biological foundation** を持つと推論できる。後の Universal Grammar 議論や biolinguistics への足場。

### 主張 2: 言語発達は運動発達と並走する maturational schedule に従う

> "However, children begin to speak no sooner and no later than when they reach a given stage of physical maturation (Table 1). ... it is not unreasonable to propose that language development, too, is related to physical growth and development. ... language development correlates better with motor development than it does with chronological age." (p.635)

Table 1 は 0.5 歳〜4.5 歳の **motor milestones（運動発達指標）** と **language milestones（言語発達指標）** を併置し、両者が一意に対応することを示す。これは「言語は学習されるのではなく、成熟する」という生物学的命題の経験的核。**5段階モデルの「波」** に直接対応する発達系列。

### 主張 3: 環境変動への頑健性（deaf parents 実験）

> "Six deaf mothers and ten hearing mothers were asked, during their last month of pregnancy, to participate in our study. ... Although the environment was quantitatively quite different in the experimental and the control groups, the frequency distributions of various baby noises did not differ significantly; ... the developmental histories of cooing noises are also remarkably alike in the two groups. ... the earliest development of human sounds appears to be relatively independent of the amount, nature, or timing of the sounds made by parents." (p.636-637)

著者自身が実施した 6 deaf mothers + 10 hearing mothers の縦断観察。生後 3 ヶ月までの cooing/fussing 頻度に有意差なし（Fig.1, 2, 3）。これは言語発達の **maturational autonomy**（成熟自律性）の実証。Skinner 派の「言語は強化学習の産物」説に対する直接反証。

### 主張 4: 双子研究は遺伝的基盤を示すが「言語の遺伝子」ではない

> "homozygotes reach milestones in language development at the same age, in contrast to heterozygotes, in whom divergences are relatively common. It is also interesting that the nature of the deviations—the symptoms, if you wish—are, in the vast majority, identical in homozygotes but not in heterozygotes." (p.638)
>
> "The endowment has a genetic foundation, but this is not to say that there are 'genes for language,' or that the environment is of no importance." (p.638)

Luchsinger の双子研究（Fig. 4）を引用しつつ、Lenneberg は **遺伝決定論を慎重に避ける**。「言語の遺伝子」ではなく、**遺伝子は細胞内生化学過程に作用し、それが間接的に発達スケジュールに影響する**という二段階の見方。これは現代 evo-devo の言語版。

### 主張 5: Critical Period — 言語獲得の臨界期

> "If a disturbance in the left hemisphere occurs early enough in life, the right hemisphere remains competent for language throughout life. Apparently this process is comparable to regulation, as we know it from morphogenesis. If the disease occurs after a certain critical period of life, namely, the early teens, this regulative capacity is lost and language is interfered with permanently." (p.639)

> "Neurological material strongly suggests that something happens in the brain during the early teens that changes the propensity for language acquisition. ... it is interesting that the critical period coincides with the time at which the human brain attains its final state of maturity in terms of structure, function, and biochemistry." (p.639)

これが本論文（および 1967 書籍）の最も影響力のある主張。**思春期早期** が言語獲得の臨界期であり、それを過ぎると左半球から右半球への regulation 能力が失われる。**5段階モデルの「縁」** に対応する境界（development → fixed function）。脳の morphogenetic regulation との同型性が指摘される点が重要。

### 主張 6: 言語は **artifact ではなく brain operation** である

> "Some investigators propose that language is an artifact—a tool that man has shaped for himself to serve a purpose. This assumption induces the view that language consists of many individual traits, each independent of the other. However, the panorama of observations presented above suggests a biological predisposition for the development of language that is anchored in the operating characteristics of the human brain." (p.640)

> "Language is to be understood as an operation rather than a static product of the mind. Its modus operandi reflects that of human cognition, because language is an intimate part of cognition. Thus the biological view denies that language is the cause of cognition, or even its effect, since language is not an object (like a tool) that exists apart from a living human brain." (p.640)

ここが Lenneberg の哲学的核。言語は **脳の operating principle そのもの** であり、ツールでも形式記号でもない。Whorf 派の「言語が思考を形成する」、Piaget 派の「思考が言語を生む」の双方を退け、**言語と認知の同根性** を主張する。これは後の embodied cognition、enactivism の伏線。

### 主張 7: Epigenesis — 段階的状態遷移の発達観

> "Maturation may be characterized as a sequence of states. At each state, the growing organism is capable of accepting some specific input; this it breaks down and resynthesizes in such a way that it makes itself develop into a new state. This new state makes the organism sensitive to new and different types of input, whose acceptance transforms it to yet a further state, which opens the way to still different input, and so on. This is called epigenesis." (p.641)

> "The child first reacts only to intonation patterns. With continued exposure to these patterns ... mechanisms develop that allow him to process the patterns ... This changes him so that he reaches a new state, a new potential for language development. Now he becomes aware of certain articulatory aspects ... A similar sequence of acceptance, synthesis, and state of new acceptance can be demonstrated on the level of semantics and syntax." (p.641)

**epigenesis** 概念。intonation → articulation → semantics → syntax の段階的状態遷移。各状態が次の状態への入力受容性を開く動態は、**5段階モデルの「渦」**（包摂・自己組織化）に対応。Waddington の epigenetic landscape の言語版。

### 主張 8: 言語普遍性は「relations of relations」

> "Virtually every aspect of language is the expression of relations. ... in all languages of the world words label a set of relational principles instead of being labels of specific objects. Knowing a word is never a simple association between an object and an acoustic pattern, but the successful operation of those principles, or application of those rules, that lead to using the word 'table' or 'house' for objects never before encountered. The language universal in this instance is not the type of object that comes to have a word, nor the particular relations involved; the universal is the generality that words stand for relations instead of being unique names for one object." (p.640)

> "Further, no language has ever been described that does not have a second order of relational principles, namely, principles in which relations are being related, that is, syntax in which relations between words are being specified. Once again, the universal is not a particular relation that occurs in all languages (though there are several such relations) but that all languages have relations of relations." (p.641)

**relations of relations** が普遍項。語は対象のラベルではなく **関係性の操作子**、文法はその二次関係。これは Jakobson（D17-S16 で同領域）の構造主義的アプローチに連なり、後の categorial grammar、type theory への足場。**5段階モデルの「束」**（多階層の関係束）に対応。

### 主張 9: 種固有性 — chimpanzee は言語化しない

> "man's biological heritage endows him with sensitivities and propensities that lead to language development in children, who are spoken to (in contrast to chimpanzee infants, who do not automatically develop language—either receptive or productive—under identical treatment)." (p.638)

> "I propose that his entire cognitive function, of which his capacity for language is an integral part, is species-specific." (p.642)

**species-specific cognitive integration**。チンパンジーの認知に「言語能力」が単純に加算されたのが人間ではなく、人間の認知全体が種固有の再統合形態を取る、という議論。これは後の Hauser-Chomsky-Fitch 2002 "Faculty of language"（FLN/FLB 区別）の伏線。

## 4. 方法論

レビュー論文（synthesis）。著者自身の経験的研究を 4 系統統合する:

1. **Cross-cultural developmental observation**: 多文化集団での言語発達速度の比較（"primitive societies" 含む）。標準化スケジュールに変動なし
2. **Deaf parents 縦断研究**: 6 deaf mothers + 10 hearing mothers の生後 3 ヶ月縦断。3 時間観察 + 24 時間機械録音、Fig.1-3 で定量
3. **Hemispherectomy / 脳損傷予後研究**: 戦傷患者・脳卒中患者・小児外科症例から、左右半球機能局在の年齢依存性を抽出
4. **遺伝的研究**: Luchsinger の双子データ（Fig.4）の二次分析

これに加えて発達生物学の概念枠組（differentiation, regulation, epigenesis）を言語獲得に転写する **conceptual transposition** が方法論的核。

**1967 書籍版との関係**: 1967 書籍は本論文の経験的根拠を全 489 頁で詳述し、Chomsky の Generative Grammar 形式論を appendix A で扱う（1969 論文 ref 11 で言及）。本 1969 要約論文は書籍の方法論と命題を維持しつつ、9 頁に圧縮した著者自身の summary。書籍を直接読まずとも、本論文で命題の核心構造は把握可能。

## 5. 5段階との対応候補

| 段階 | 対応候補 | 強度 | 原文引用 |
|------|---------|------|---------|
| 1 場 (Field) | 脳の operating principles 全体 / cognitive integration の場としての species-specific cognition | 中 | "language is to be understood as an operation rather than a static product of the mind. Its modus operandi reflects that of human cognition" (p.640); "his entire cognitive function ... is species-specific" (p.642) |
| 2 波 (Wave) | maturational schedule に沿う発達系列。intonation → articulation → semantics → syntax の段階的展開 | 強 | "language development, too, is related to physical growth and development. ... language development correlates better with motor development than it does with chronological age" (p.635); Table 1 motor-language milestones |
| 3 縁 (Relation) | critical period 境界（early teens）。左右半球差化、regulative capacity の喪失境界 | 強 | "the critical period coincides with the time at which the human brain attains its final state of maturity ... the maturation of the brain marks the end of regulation and locks certain functions into place" (p.639); "language acquisition becomes more difficult when the physical maturation of the brain is complete" (p.635) |
| 4 渦 (Vortex) | epigenesis = 各状態が次の状態への入力受容性を開く動態。状態遷移の自己組織化 | 強 | "Maturation may be characterized as a sequence of states. At each state, the growing organism is capable of accepting some specific input; this it breaks down and resynthesizes in such a way that it makes itself develop into a new state" (p.641) |
| 5 束 (Bundle) | language universals = relations of relations。多階層の関係束として言語が成立 | 強 | "no language has ever been described that does not have a second order of relational principles ... all languages have relations of relations" (p.641); "words label a set of relational principles instead of being labels of specific objects" (p.640) |

**判定基準についての注記**:

- **Stage 2 波・Stage 3 縁・Stage 4 渦・Stage 5 束 が四本柱で「強」**。これが本論文を D17 において Tier1 にする根拠。発達系列（波）、臨界期（縁）、epigenesis（渦）、関係性の関係性（束）が 1969 論文の 9 頁内に明示的に揃う
- Stage 1 場（脳の operating principles）は中。"operation rather than a static product"（p.640）の表現はあるが、5段階の「場」が意味する「未分化の沃野」とはニュアンスが異なる。脳の生物学的基盤を言語の生成場として捉える点で中程度の対応
- 1967 書籍版を直接読めば、特に Stage 1（脳の生化学的基盤）と Stage 4（regulation/morphogenesis の詳細）の論証密度がさらに高まる可能性。但し 1969 要約論文だけで全段階「強」または「中-強」が確認できる
- D17-S12 Kirby et al. 2008 と並んで D17 領域で **構造類似最も鮮明な原典の一つ**。Kirby が経験的（10 世代伝達連鎖）に 5段階を実証したのに対し、本論文は概念的・発達生物学的に同じ構造を提示

## 6. 限界・留意事項

- **1967 書籍直接精読の不在**: 本ノートは 1969 Science 要約論文（9 頁）に基づく。1967 書籍 489 頁の経験データの詳細・脳神経科学的論証の細部・Chomsky 生成文法 appendix の評価は未確認。ただし著者自身が "complete treatment" として書籍を参照する構造から、1969 論文の命題群は書籍の核心と等価
- **Critical Period 概念の現代的批判**: 後の研究（Newport, Birdsong 等）で Lenneberg の「思春期で硬い境界」説は緩和されている（**graded sensitivity** 説）。Hartshorne et al. 2018（D17-S10）はオンライン調査で sharp drop を報告するが、Lenneberg の物理的基盤と直結しないニュアンス
- **methodologically dated**: 1969 当時の脳画像技術・神経生理測定は現代水準より粗い。電気生理（EEG）と神経解剖学的論証の混在
- **species-specific 主張の論拠**: チンパンジー言語実験（Washoe, Sarah, Nim）は 1969 当時はまだ未完。Lenneberg の chimpanzee 論は予測的命題で、その後 ASL 訓練実験で部分的に修正される（生成文法ではなく語彙レベルの学習は可能）
- **遺伝-環境二項対立の慎重な回避**: 「言語の遺伝子」を否定する箇所は明示的だが、後続研究（FOXP2 等）の特定遺伝子研究は当時未着手
