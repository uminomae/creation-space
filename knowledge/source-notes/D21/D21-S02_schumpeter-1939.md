- **出典**: Volume I, McGraw-Hill Book Company Inc., New York and London, 1939（本 PDF の刊記には "First published August 1923, Reprinted 1939" とあるが通説ではオリジナル 1939 年、本書は 1939 年版）

## 2. 要旨（読んだ内容に基づく）

Schumpeter 1939 は 1912 年 *Theorie der wirtschaftlichen Entwicklung*（D21-S01）の発展版であり、1 人の企業者による「新結合」というミクロ分析を、(a) 企業者の群生（clustering）、(b) 資本主義経済の波動（undulating/wavelike movement）、(c) 多重スケールの景気循環（three-cycle schema: Kondratieff 長波・Juglar 中波・Kitchin 短波）へと拡張する。核心命題は「景気循環とは資本主義の経済過程の本質であり、それは循環の病ではなく成長の形式である」こと。分析は以下 3 層からなる: First Approximation（Pure Model, 2 段階: 均衡離脱 → 新均衡接近）、Second Approximation（Secondary Wave と 4 段階循環）、Third Approximation（3 種循環の重畳）。本書の統計・歴史部分は 1787 年以降の英・米・独経済を対象とし、Industrial Revolution 以降の長波 (Kondratieff) を歴史的に跡づける。

## 3. 主要主張（原文引用付き、選読セクションに基づく）

### 主張 1: 資本主義経済進化の核心は「Innovation」であり、「Invention」とは別概念

> "3. By changes in the methods of supplying commodities we mean a range of events much broader than the phrase covers in its literal acceptance. We include the introduction of new commodities which may even serve as the standard case. Technological change in the production of commodities already in use, the opening up of new markets or of new sources of supply, Taylorization of work, improved handling of material, the setting up of new business organizations such as department stores — in short, any 'doing things differently' in the realm of economic life — all these are instances of what we shall refer to by the term Innovation." (p.84)

> "It should be noticed at once that that concept is not synonymous with 'invention.' [...] the making of the invention and the carrying out of the corresponding innovation are, economically and sociologically, two entirely different things." (pp.84-85)

> "innovation is readily seen to be a distinct internal factor of change. It is an internal factor because the turning of existing factors of production to new uses is a purely economic process [...] It is a distinct internal factor because it is not implied in, nor a mere consequence of, any other." (p.86)

Innovation は新商品・新技術だけでなく、新市場開拓・新供給源・新組織形式・Taylor 式労働管理など「経済生活での何かを違うようにすること」全てを含む。Invention（発明）は技術的・知的過程で社会過程・心理特性が異なり、Innovation と一致するのは偶然に過ぎない。Innovation は内因的・独立的変化要因として Growth（成長）や Tastes（嗜好変化）とは原理的に区別される。

### 主張 2: Innovation の厳密な定義 — 生産関数の形式変更

> "We will now define innovation more rigorously by means of the production function previously introduced. As we know, this function describes the way in which quantity of product varies if quantities of factors vary. If, instead of quantities of factors, we vary the form of the function, we have an innovation. [...] we will simply define innovation as the setting up of a new production function." (p.87)

> "Innovation breaks off any such 'curve' and replaces it by another which, again except for indivisibility, displays higher increments of product throughout, although, of course, it also decreases monotonically. [...] In both cases transition is made by a jump from the old to the new curve, which now applies throughout and not only beyond that output which had been produced before by the old method." (p.88)

Innovation は生産関数の「形式（form）」変更として定量化される。これは従来の限界生産性曲線に沿った量的変化ではなく、曲線そのものの置換（jump）として現れる。

### 主張 3: 経済進化は「均衡近傍 → 均衡離脱 → 新均衡接近」の 2 段階 Pure Model で理解できる

> "It will be useful to assemble the analytic tools so far described and to display the resulting skeleton — a sort of chassis of our model. [...] We assume (this, however, is no assumption) that, in the institutional pattern of capitalist society, there will always be possibilities of New Combinations [...] and always some people able and willing to carry them out; to repeat again a point which has often been misunderstood, these people are by no means looked upon as particularly rare birds. All we postulate is that that ability is distributed as unequally as others are and all we hold is that this fact has an important influence on the mechanism of economic change [...]" (p.130)

> "Then other entrepreneurs still follow, after them still others in increasing number, in the path of innovation, which becomes progressively smoothed for successors by accumulating experience and vanishing obstacles." (p.131)

> "This process of economic change or evolution, moreover, goes on in units separated from each other by neighborhoods of equilibrium. Each of those units, in turn, consists of two distinct phases, during the first of which the system, under the impulse of entrepreneurial activity, draws away from an equilibrium position, and during the second of which it draws toward another equilibrium position. [...] that very sequence of events which we observe in the course of those fluctuations in economic life which have come to be called business cycles, and which, translated into the language of diagrams, present the picture of an undulating or wavelike movement in absolute figures or rates of change." (p.138)

Pure Model（First Approximation）は 2 段階構造: (1) 均衡近傍からの逸脱 — 先駆企業者の新結合実行 → 続く追随群の急増、(2) 新均衡への接近 — autodeflation + 価値の再評価 + 不適応企業の淘汰 + 残存企業の再編成。この 2 段階が「業績循環（business cycle）の波動として観察される」。

### 主張 4: 企業者の群生 (clustering) と「新しい経済空間」の創出

> "the new methods or commodities create new Economic Space. But for others the emergence of the new methods means economic death; for still others, contraction and drifting into the background. Finally, there are firms and industries which are forced to undergo a difficult and painful process of modernization, rationalization and reconstruction." (p.134)

> "But as the process gathers momentum, these effects steadily gain in importance and disequilibrium, enforcing a process of adaptation, begins to show." (p.134)

先駆者の後に追随する企業者群は、経験累積と障害減退により加速的に増加する（「群生」）。既存企業は (1) 新機会として拡張する、(2) 経済的死に至る、(3) 背景化する、(4) 苦痛を伴う近代化・合理化・再構成を強いられる — 4 つの分岐的運命をたどる。Innovation は「新しい経済空間（new Economic Space）」を創り、古い空間を部分的に破壊する。

### 主張 5: 「進歩」は本質的に非安定化的であり、周期過程として現象する

> "First, it is by no means farfetched or paradoxical to say that 'progress' unstabilizes the economic world, or that it is by virtue of its mechanism a cyclical process. A theory of economic fluctuations running in terms of external factors plus innovations might be considered self-evident and only another way of stating that there would be no cycles in an undisturbed stationary, or growing, flow." (p.138)

> "Our proposition that innovation — again, when seen in its true extent and not confined to some part or form of what we mean by it — is actually the dominant element which accounts for those historical and statistical phenomena, is so far only a working hypothesis, which will be on trial throughout this book." (p.139)

> "We acquire the right to look upon recession as the reaction to prosperity in the way first clearly recognized by Juglar, without having in turn to explain prosperity by preceding recession." (p.139)

景気循環は「進歩に対する異常」ではなく「進歩そのものの動的形式」。革新（innovation）が景気循環の原因であり、不況（recession）は好況に対する必然的反応 — 不況を説明するために別の外因を持ち出す必要はない。

### 主張 6: 三重循環スキーマ（TOC + 表題から要約）

> **TOC:**
> "C. The Secondary Wave; Second Approximation [...] Secondary effects induced by the fundamental process: depression and the four-phase cycle [...]
> D. Many Simultaneous Cycles; Third Approximation [...] The historical development of the problem of cycles [...] the 'discovery' of long and short waves. Derivation of the three-cycle schema [...] 1. The three-cycle schema not another hypothesis. 2. Motives of the decision to work with three cycles. 3. Relations between the Kondratieffs, Juglars, and Kitchins." (pp.x, 145, 161-174)

Ch. IV の Third Approximation では、景気循環を単一波ではなく複数スケールの波の重ね合わせとして記述: **Kondratieff 長波（約 45-60 年）、Juglar 中波（約 7-11 年）、Kitchin 短波（約 3-4 年）**。それぞれが独立した innovation のクラスターに対応し、歴史的・統計的 evidence が重ねられる（本読解では Ch.IV D は TOC から構造のみ把握、本文は未精読）。

## 4. 方法論

Schumpeter 1939 の方法論は 3 層構造:

1. **理論モデルの段階的精緻化**（First → Second → Third Approximation）: Pure Model（2 段階）→ Secondary Wave と 4 段階循環 → Kondratieff/Juglar/Kitchin 3 種循環の重畳。各近似は前の近似の単純化を現実に即して段階的に緩める
2. **歴史分析**（Ch. VI-VII Historical Outlines）: Industrial Revolution 前（1787 年以前）の 300 年、第一次 Kondratieff 期（1787-1842）、第二次 Kondratieff 期（1843-1913）の 3 つの長期を英・米・独経済について詳述
3. **統計分析**（Ch. V Time Series and Their Normal）: 時系列の「Trend」と「Cycle」の分離法、normal points 法（Frisch の方法）、合成時系列の扱い

本書の「理論・歴史・統計の交雑（mutual peaceful penetration）」が方法論的特徴であり、Schumpeter は序文で「analytical principles are my own」とし 1912 年の理論的骨格 (scaffolding) を「家屋 (house)」に仕上げたと述べる (Preface, p.v)。

## 5. 5段階との対応候補

| 段階 | 対応候補 | 強度 | 原文引用 |
|------|---------|------|---------|
| 1 場 (Field) | 静態均衡（stationary circuit flow / Theoretical Norm） — innovation 前の未分化な循環的定常状態。全員が経験に基づき習慣的に行動する | 強 | "Starting from a stationary state in perfect equilibrium, exclusion of savings as a major factor in bringing about that change follows logically [...]" (p.83); "starting from a state of perfect equilibrium from which to start. There is no saving, population is constant, and everything else is as we assume it to be in a state that conforms to the idea of the Theoretical Norm." (p.130) |
| 2 波 (Wave) | 企業者の新結合実行による impulse — 均衡からの逸脱を開始する最初の innovation 実行 | 強 | "Some people, then, conceive and work out with varying promptness plans for innovations associated with varying (and ideally correct) anticipations of profits, and set about struggling with the obstacles incident to doing a new and unfamiliar thing [...]" (p.130); "during the first of which the system, under the impulse of entrepreneurial activity, draws away from an equilibrium position" (p.138) |
| 3 縁 (Relation) | 「新しい経済空間」と「既存経済」の界面 — 旧生産関数と新生産関数の境界で、企業淘汰・合理化・近代化が迫られる領域 | 強 | "For some of the 'old' firms new opportunities for expansion open up: the new methods or commodities create new Economic Space. But for others the emergence of the new methods means economic death; for still others, contraction and drifting into the background. Finally, there are firms and industries which are forced to undergo a difficult and painful process of modernization, rationalization and reconstruction." (p.134) |
| 4 渦 (Vortex) | 4 段階循環として個として立ち上がる景気循環（好況 → 後退 → 不況 → 回復）— 「恐慌の病」ではなく「成長の形式」として閉じた個別単位 | 強 | "This process of economic change or evolution, moreover, goes on in units separated from each other by neighborhoods of equilibrium. Each of those units, in turn, consists of two distinct phases [...]" (p.138); "Secondary effects induced by the fundamental process: depression and the four-phase cycle" (TOC, p.x); "recession as the reaction to prosperity" (p.139) |
| 5 束 (Bundle) | 三重循環スキーマ（Kondratieff + Juglar + Kitchin）としての資本主義の時間構造 — 長中短の波が入れ子状に重なる universal pattern | 強 | "D. Many Simultaneous Cycles; Third Approximation [...] The historical development of the problem of cycles [...] 'discovery' of long and short waves. Derivation of the three-cycle schema [...] Relations between the Kondratieffs, Juglars, and Kitchins." (TOC, p.x); "The subject will be taken up later on." (p.139, footnote re: depression/prosperity cycle overlay) |

**判定基準**:
- **強**: 著者が直接その概念・構造を論じており、5段階の定義と明確に対応する
- **弱**: 構造的類似はあるが、著者の文脈・意図とは異なる読みである
- **なし**: 対応が見出せない。無理に作らない

**注記**:
- **全 5 段階が「強」対応** という稀有な事例。これは 5段階モデルが Schumpeter の景気循環理論を陰に参照し得ることを示唆するが、牽強付会の疑いを排除するため各対応の根拠を厳密化した
- 特に **Stage 5（束）の三重循環スキーマ** は、複数スケールの循環波が同時に走り重畳する構造を明示しており、「束」概念の経済学的実現として最強の例
- Stage 1 （場）は静態循環 (stationary circuit flow) として 1912 年 *Theorie* D21-S01 の「Kreislauf」概念と同じ。両書を併読すべき
- Stage 2 （波）と Stage 4（渦）の区別は、Schumpeter の "impulse"（波）と "cycle unit"（渦）の区別に対応。impulse は離散的な逸脱の始まり、cycle unit は両 phase を含む完結した自己反復単位
- **manifest ヒントからの独立性**: 1939 年版は 1912 年版の拡張だが、「循環」と「群生」概念の明示は 1939 年に強化された独自貢献。1912 年の Stage 5 が「なし」だった（D21-S01 §5）のに対し、1939 年では「強」に昇格する点が重要
