## 2. 要旨（読んだ内容に基づく）

政治学者は政治過程を "path dependent" と形容することが増えているが、この概念の明確な定式化は稀である。Pierson は経済学における "increasing returns" の dynamic (Arthur 1994, David 1985) を path dependence の核と同定し、その政治学への移植を体系的に行う。Increasing returns とは、ある選択肢を採ることで次に同じ選択肢を採る確率が上がる positive feedback 過程を指し、4 つの特徴を生む: (1) unpredictability (初期条件の小さな差が異なる均衡を生む)、(2) inflexibility (時間経過で別経路への切替えコストが急増)、(3) nonergodicity (初期の小さな出来事が大きな結果に長期的影響)、(4) potential path inefficiency (lock-in された選択肢が最適でない場合がある)。Pierson はさらに、**政治**は経済より path dependence に脆弱であると論じる — 理由は 4 つ: (i) 集合行為の中心性、(ii) 制度的高密度、(iii) 政治権力の非対称性強化機能、(iv) 政治過程の複雑性と不透明性。これにより政治学は "historical institutionalism" の理論的基盤を得る。Critical junctures, increasing returns, timing and sequencing を分析焦点に据える方法論的含意を提示。

## 3. 主要主張（原文引用付き）

### 主張 1: Path dependence の厳密な定式化の必要性

> "The concept, however, is often employed without careful elaboration. This article conceptualizes path dependence as a social process grounded in a dynamic of 'increasing returns.'"

Levi (1997) の 'ツリーと枝' 比喩: "From the same trunk, there are many different branches and smaller branches. Although it is possible to turn around or to clamber from one to the other—and essential if the chosen branch dies—the branch on which a climber begins is the one she tends to follow."

Pierson は loose な "history matters" 論ではなく、increasing returns に基づく narrow な定式化を支持する。

### 主張 2: Polya urn process — Path dependence の数理的イメージ

> "Imagine a very large urn containing two balls, one black, one red. Remove one ball, and then return it to the urn, accompanied by an additional ball of the same color. Repeat this process until the urn fills up."

> "Mathematicians call this a Polya urn process. Its characteristic qualities stem from the fact that an element of chance (or accident) is combined with a decision rule that links current probabilities to the outcomes of preceding (partly random) sequences."

この思考実験で path dependence の本質 (早期の random 事象が stable equilibrium を決定) を視覚化。各軌道の「vicious/virtuous cycle」が自己強化的に確立される。

### 主張 3: Increasing returns の 4 つの特徴 (Arthur 1994)

> "1. Unpredictability. Because early events have a large effect and are partly random, many outcomes may be possible."
> "2. Inflexibility. The farther into the process we are, the harder it becomes to shift from one path to another."
> "3. Nonergodicity. Accidental events early in a sequence do not cancel out (which is to say, ignored) as 'noise,' because they feed back into future choices."
> "4. Potential path inefficiency. In the long-run, the outcome that becomes locked in may generate lower pay-offs than a forgone alternative would have."

後段の political analysis を方向付ける 4 features。

### 主張 4: Increasing returns を生む 4 つの技術的・社会的 feature (Arthur 1994)

> "1. Large set-up or fixed costs. These create a high pay-off for further investments in a given technology."
> "2. Learning effects. Knowledge gained in the operation of complex systems also leads to higher returns from continuing use."
> "3. Coordination effects. These occur when the benefits an individual receives from a particular activity increase as others adopt the same option."
> "4. Adaptive expectations. If options that fail to win broad acceptance early on, then individuals may feel a need to 'pick the right horse'."

QWERTY, VHS vs Betamax, DOS vs Macintosh など technology lock-in の例は、こうした conditions が存在する場合の自然な帰結。

### 主張 5: 政治は経済より path dependence に脆弱 — 4 特性

Pierson の central contribution。経済では market mechanism (efficient competition, learning, fast correction) が path dependence を緩和するが、政治では対応する機構が弱い:

> "Three characteristics of politics change the picture considerably: the absence or weakness of efficiency-enhancing mechanisms of competition and learning; the shorter time horizons of political actors; and the strong status quo bias generally built into political institutions."

#### 5-a. Collective action の中心性
> "A crucial feature of most collective action in politics is the absence of a linear relationship between effort and effect. Instead, collective action frequently involves many of the qualities conducive to positive feedback (Marwell and Oliver 1993)."

"When picking the wrong horse may have very high costs, actors must constantly adjust their behavior in the light of how they expect others to act." — political parties や social movements の persistent dynamics を説明。

#### 5-b. Institutional density of politics
> "Once established, these institutional constraints can apply to all—those who do not approve as well as those who do—and they are backed up, ultimately, by force. The exit option, so central to the workings of the market, is often unavailable (or prohibitively costly) to actors who feel poorly served by existing political arrangements."

Exit option の欠如が lock-in を強化。政策・憲法的ルールの reversibility は市場商品より遥かに低い。

#### 5-c. Political authority and power asymmetries
> "Increasing returns processes can transform a situation of relatively balanced conflict, in which one set of actors must openly impose its preferences on another set ('the first face of power'), into one in which power relations become so uneven that anticipated reactions ('the second face of power') and ideological manipulation ('the third face') make open political conflict unnecessary."

権力の非対称性自体が positive feedback 過程に服する。

#### 5-d. Complexity and opacity
> "Politics is a far, far murkier environment [than economics]. It lacks anything like the measuring rod of price. Political actors pursue a range of goals. Furthermore, it is often very hard to observe or measure important aspects of political performance."

情報の不透明性により learning と correction が遅く、path dependence を緩和する自己修正機構が働きにくい。

### 主張 6: Time horizons の重要性 — 政治家は短期志向

> "A statement attributed to David Stockman, budget director during the Reagan administration, is unusual among political decision makers only for its candor. Asked by an adviser in 1981 to address Social Security's severe long-term financing problems, Stockman dismissed the idea out of hand. He explained that he had little interest in wasting 'a lot of political capital on some other guy's problem in [the year] 2010' (quoted in Greider 1982, 43)."

政治アクターの time horizon は選挙サイクル (2-4 年) に縛られ、long-term cost 評価が systematically 歪められる。これが path-dependent lock-in を加速。

### 主張 7: Path dependence in politics が持つ 4 つの特徴 (Pierson まとめ)

> "1. Multiple equilibria. Under a set of initial conditions conducive to increasing returns, a number of outcomes—perhaps a wide range—are generally possible."
> "2. Contingency. Relatively small events, if they occur at the right moment, can have large and enduring consequences."
> "3. A critical role for timing and sequencing."
> "4. Inertia. Once an increasing returns process is established, positive feedback may lead to a single equilibrium."

これらは社会科学における "historical" explanation の方法論的基盤となる。

### 主張 8: Historical Institutionalism への含意

> "There are, then, compelling reasons to believe that political life will often be marked by dynamics of increasing returns. Tendencies toward positive feedback characterize four processes central to political environments: collective action, institutional development, the exercise of authority, and social interpretation."

Collier & Collier (1991), Skocpol (1992), Ikenberry (1994), Krasner (1989) の先行研究が critical junctures や historical causation を扱ってきたのに対し、Pierson はその理論的 foundation を increasing returns に求める。

## 4. 方法論

- **概念論文**: 新規データ提示なく、path dependence 概念の理論的整理
- **経済学からの移入と改変**: Arthur (1994), David (1985), North (1990) の increasing returns 議論を政治学に適応
- **Liebowitz-Margolis (1995) 批判との対決**: "remediable" vs "nonremediable" path dependence の区別を検討し、政治領域では前者の妥当性も保持される理由を論じる
- **比較議論**: 経済の市場機構 (competition, learning) が path dependence を緩和するメカニズムと、政治のそれら mechanism の弱さを対比
- **具体例**: Polya urn, QWERTY, Pattern of political institutions, Social Security, party system, cognitive maps (Denzau & North 1994), voluntary associations (Skocpol 1999)

## 5. 5段階との対応候補

| 段階 | 対応候補 | 強度 | 原文引用 |
|------|---------|------|---------|
| 1 場 (Field) | "Set of initial conditions conducive to increasing returns" — path dependence が作動する制度環境と条件の場 | 強 | "Under a set of initial conditions conducive to increasing returns, a number of outcomes—perhaps a wide range—are generally possible." |
| 2 波 (Wave) | Polya urn process の stochastic sampling、contingent timing of events | 中 | "Accidental events early in a sequence do not cancel out (which is to say, ignored) as 'noise,' because they feed back into future choices." |
| 3 縁 (Relation) | Positive feedback (increasing returns) における「個別選択が他の選択に与える影響」の関係構造、制度間 coordination effects | 強 | "Coordination effects ... occur when the benefits an individual receives from a particular activity increase as others adopt the same option." |
| 4 渦 (Vortex) | Self-reinforcing virtuous/vicious cycle、path lock-in の自己強化ループ | 強 | "As such effects begin to accumulate, they generate a powerful virtuous (or vicious) cycle of self-reinforcing activity." |
| 5 束 (Bundle) | Institutional matrix — 複数の institutions/policies/cognitive maps が一体化して "frozen" した政治秩序 | 強 | "The interdependent web of an institutional matrix produces massive increasing returns" (North 1990 引用) |

**判定基準**:
- Stage 1 (強): Pierson は「どういう conditions が増分収益を生むか」を明示的に場として定式化する (Arthur の 4 feature)
- Stage 2 (中): Polya urn の random sampling として波的 contingency を扱うが、「波動」そのものを論じるのではない
- Stage 3 (強): Individual choice と他者の選択との coordination effects を明示、これは典型的な「縁」
- Stage 4 (強): Virtuous/vicious cycle を明示的に self-reinforcing loop として記述
- Stage 5 (強): North の institutional matrix は「束ね」のアーキタイプ、多数要素が噛み合って一体化したシステム

## 6. 限界・留意事項

- **概念論文**: 実証データは既存研究からの引用で、独自のケーススタディはなし。QWERTY など有名例への依存
- **Increasing returns の量的測定困難**: 政治現象における "learning effects" や "coordination effects" の厳密な定量化は未解決
- **対抗視点**: Liebowitz & Margolis (1995) の批判 (path dependence は remediable ならば重要ではない) に対する Pierson の反論は、政治領域では remediable でも意義があるという主張に留まる
- **Small-n 問題**: 比較歴史分析での "critical junctures" 識別は n が小さいため統計的検定が困難
- **Multiple equilibria の identify 困難**: 複数均衡の存在を ex ante 判定する方法は提示されない
- **初期条件の endogeneity**: path dependence 議論は初期条件を外生的に扱うが、実際にはそれ自身が先行する歴史的過程の産物である
- **2000 年時点**: Mahoney (2000), Thelen (1999) の並行作業との関係や、その後の gradual institutional change 議論 (Streeck & Thelen 2005) は本論文の射程外
