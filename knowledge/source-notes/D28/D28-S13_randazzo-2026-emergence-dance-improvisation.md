## 2. 要旨（読んだ内容に基づく）

本論文は「即興ダンスにおいて『効果性 (effectiveness)』を語ることは可能か」という問いを起点に、創発 (emergence) を即興における芸術的創造性の「パラメータ」として位置付ける哲学論文である。即興は定義上予測不可能であり、また即興者は既存の動きの語彙を showcase することが目的ではないため、通常「effective かどうか」という評価軸は退けられるとされてきた。Randazzo はこれに対し、創発概念に二つの形態 — (i) 出来事としての創発 (emergence as occurrence) を枠組とする「枠組 for emergence (framework for emergence)」と、(ii) 下向因果 (downward causation) としての創発 を枠組とする「立ち現れる枠組 (emerging framework)」— を区別し、それぞれが異なる意味での「効果性」と「芸術的創造性」を生むと論証する。前者は身体の習慣パターンを disrupt して動きの語彙を拡張するという意味での効果性・創造性、後者は即興中に集団的合意によって可能性の場を縮減し、形と意味の一貫した統一を生むという意味での効果性・創造性である (Abstract, p.33-34)。議論の主素材は 1960-70 年代アメリカの postmodern ダンス (Judson Dance Theater) と、現代ヨーロッパの即興実践者 (David Zambrano, João Fiadeiro, Emma Bigé, Laura Aris Álvarez, Anna Halprin, Simone Forti, Frédéric Pouillaude 等) である (Sec.2, p.35)。

## 3. 主要主張（原文引用付き）

### 主張 1: 即興における「効果性」は通常の意味では成立しないが、別の意味では成立する

> "When discussing artistic improvisation, effectiveness is generally dismissed for at least two main reasons. On the one hand, an improviser cannot anticipate the appropriate means to achieve an end that, by definition, they do not set in advance—hence the inherent risk of failure. On the other hand, an improviser does not aim to showcase their virtuosity in appropriating a particular movement vocabulary, insofar as their intention is rather to circumvent habitual patterns and expand their vocabulary. The aim of this article, however, is to demonstrate that invoking the notion of effectiveness is not incompatible with the indeterminate nature of improvisation." (Abstract, p.33)

> "our thesis is that emergence—implying a sudden appearance of novelty—can be seen as a key parameter in fostering the effectiveness of artistic creativity in an improvisation." (Abstract, p.33)

即興者は目的を事前に設定しないため通常の「手段—目的適合」という効果性概念は不適用だが、創発を鍵とする別種の効果性概念が成立する、という論文全体の命題がここで提示される。

### 主張 2: 即興には枠組が必要である — 「すべてが可能 = 何も起こらない」の逆説

Sec.3.1 で Randazzo は、完全な自由には逆説的に創造性が宿らないと論じ、David Zambrano の実践報告を引用する。

> "what happens is that many times when I go to teach improvisation, for many people, young people especially, it's like they can do whatever (they want). But you can say: 'Where are we going?' and they answer: 'I don't care, whatever', and they stay here. And then I say, 'okay, it's two of us, where are we going?', and they answer back 'I don't care, every place'. In that case, there is not really a place that we can go…" (Zambrano, quoted p.6, Sec.3.1, p.36)

> "The first reason is that, without a defined framework for action, nothing may occur. The simultaneous presence of too many possibilities can, in fact, lead to a paralysis of action." (Sec.3.1, p.36)

> "In this context, it may be fruitful to speak of 'frameworks for emergence' to refer to such frameworks for action established prior to improvisation." (Sec.3.1, p.36)

事前の枠組 (action guidelines, constraints) が即興に方向性と触発を与える。これが「framework for emergence」と呼ばれる。

### 主張 3: Framework for emergence は習慣パターンを disrupt するための「enabling constraints」である

> "These guidelines may involve, for instance, a movement constraint. For example, Anna Halprin asked participants to move on the ground without using arms or legs. This encourages the exploration of novel ways to move using the spine. [...] These action guidelines can also involve a constraint on the quality of movement. To take one example among many, Simone Forti explores in her *Land Portraits* how the dynamic quality of a landscape can permeate her movements as well as those of her group" (Sec.3.2, p.37)

> "These constraints are not, nor do they remain, disabling (though they initially appear to be so by disrupting our motor but also sensory, and cognitive habits). They are rather 'enabling constraints' because they open possibilities, particularly regarding 'how' an action may be performed." (Sec.3.3, p.37, citing Erin Manning's distinction)

習慣の disruption こそが enabling の条件である、という逆説的構造を Randazzo は明示的に述べる。

### 主張 4: Framework for emergence における効果性 = 動きの語彙の拡張

> "Therefore, if there is effectiveness here, it does not refer to an action that produces an expected (and often considered useful) outcome by assessing the appropriateness of the means that are used, i.e., the appropriateness of the 'how' of the action. Effectiveness here refers rather to the ease with which these frameworks for action can give rise to movements that are unfamiliar to the dancers—that is, to their capacity to stimulate imagination and artistic creativity. In other words, the effectiveness of these frameworks is understood in terms of the openness and richness of the possibilities they enable. [...] The fluidity of movements as they follow and call to one another can also be perceived by viewers." (Sec.3.3, p.37)

> "In sum, the emergence that 'arises from' the initially established action guidelines, set for improvisation, can be regarded as a parameter of artistic creativity's effectiveness. The precision and openness of these guidelines indeed generate a form of creativity understood as an expansion and enrichment of one's movement vocabulary." (Sec.3.3, p.37)

この第一の効果性は、未知の動きへと身体を開く「創発 as occurrence」の機能による。

### 主張 5: しかし即興の本質にはさらに「立ち現れる枠組 (emerging framework)」という第二段階がある

Sec.4.1 で Randazzo は Anna Halprin の「exploration vs. improvisation」の区別を引く。

> "dance explorations involve exploring all the possibilities of a specific idea (e.g., running through the dance studio while paying attention to the different directions given to our spine). This is how new 'resources' can be brought out from the body. But while 'explorations' require staying on the same task, 'improvisations' always move on to something else, to new directions. That's why she says that 'explorations' are more precise and controlled." (Sec.4.1, p.38)

> "However, regarding here on emergence, it is particularly interesting to consider what is at stake when an improviser chooses to move on to something else. While it is true that in 'explorations' there is already improvisation—since the movements are not conceived prior to their execution—it is not yet, strictly speaking, an 'improvisation'. Improvisation, properly understood, involves in fact a shift in operation. It implies the deconstruction of a given framework that is then restructured in a different way. In this sense, what is truly said to be 'improvisation' occurs when a 'framework for emergence' (as previously explained) becomes, properly speaking, an 'emerging framework' co-constructed in real time between practitioners." (Sec.4.1, p.38)

即興の核心は、予め設定された枠組 (framework for emergence) からの「deconstruction → restructuring」という移行である。練習者達が real-time で共構築する枠組が「emerging framework」と呼ばれる。

### 主張 6: Emerging framework は下向因果 (downward causation) の形をとる

Sec.4.3 で Randazzo は emergence の下向因果としての側面を Jaegwon Kim を引いて説明する。

> "Despite the fact that the concept of emergence can be applicable to many fields, it is commonly defined as a whole, greater than the sum of its parts, whose underlying elements and properties constitute the base for the emergent phenomenon. As Jaegwon Kim states, 'an emergent property is new because it is unpredictable, and this is its epistemological sense; and, second, in a metaphysical sense, namely that an emergent property brings with it new causal powers, power that did not exist before its emergence'." (Sec.4.3, p.39, Kim quoted)

> "emergence implies both an extension and a negation, both a continuation and an irreducibility between the base and the emergent elements. That's why it fosters something new." (Sec.4.3, p.39)

Emerging framework は、練習者間の interaction から立ち上がり、その後「以前には存在しなかった新しい因果力」として既存の action guidelines を上書きする。この上書きこそが下向因果である。

### 主張 7: Emerging framework における効果性 = リアルタイムで可能性を縮減して集合的一貫性を生む能力

> "On the other hand, the effectiveness of an emergence as downward causation (with regard to an 'emerging framework') is ensured by the capacity to reduce the range of possibilities thanks to collectively made choices aimed at serving the situation that is unfolding. This fosters artistic creativity again, newly understood as the creation of collective norms, and newly manifested through the emergence of a coherent unity of form and meaning among practitioners." (Sec.5, p.41)

> "In this regard, R. Keith Sawyer highlights the 'interactional framework' he theorizes: 'The more complex the frameworks become, the greater the interactional power of the emergent framework, thereby reducing the range of possible actions'" (Sawyer quoted, p.41)

第一の効果性が「拡張」（動きの語彙を広げる）なのに対し、第二の効果性は「縮減」（可能性の場を絞って一貫性を作る）である。両者は対立ではなく連続した 2 局面として作動する。

### 主張 8: 即興は allotelic（目的が外にある）ではなく autotelic（自己目的的）である

> "Finally, this tendency of the initial framework to exceed itself sheds light on these 'frameworks for emergence'. Indeed, these are structures that may be referred to as 'allotelic', as opposed to 'autotelic' structures: their purpose lies outside of themselves. To put it differently, they find 'their fulfillment in their own transcendence', and they aim to 'transcend their own frameworks', bringing about a realization that exceeds the initial possibilities of the action (our translation)." (Sec.4.2, p.39)

即興の枠組は自己外在的な目的（枠組そのものの超越）を持ち、そのため framework は自らを否定することで創発を完成させる。これが Randazzo の議論の核心にある構造論である。

### 主張 9: 即興的創造性は「depersonalized creativity」である — 自己からの後退

Sec.6 で Randazzo は即興的創造性の本質論に移行する。

> "creativity was linked to the enrichment of our movement vocabulary and, consequently, to the enhancement of the experience of our own body. [...] But we also observed in a second phase that improvisation gave rise to a collective creativity, evidently through the real-time emergence of new action guidelines and through the unfolding of a shared and meaningful coherence of forms across the practitioners' movements. More precisely, as previously discussed, these norms emerge from collectively negotiated choices aimed at responding to the dynamic demands of the situation. In other words, reaching such collective choices does not involve merely adding up individual decisions or summing individual intentions. It simply requires being attentive and willing to serve the demands of a situation in the making. Consequently, collective creativity may be conceived as 'an expression of the milieu'" (Sec.6, p.42)

> "In conclusion, some artists and/or theorists emphasize the *ineffectiveness* of improvisation, partly because it exposes itself to the risk of failure, and partly because the improviser is not intended to perform skillful feats. But we have sought throughout this article to demonstrate how the processes of emergence at play in improvisation can, on the contrary, foster the effectiveness of artistic creativity. [...] These considerations ultimately led us to reconceptualize artistic creativity. In improvisation, indeed, it is no longer a matter of personal creation or psychological expression. It is rather a depersonalized creativity that is facilitated by the action guidelines of the initial framework for action. And it is also a collective creativity that takes the form of an 'expression of the milieu', aiming to best serve the demands of an ongoing situation." (Sec.7 Conclusions, p.43)

Randazzo は創造性概念を「個人的表現」から「milieu の表現」「depersonalized creativity」へと脱中心化することで論文を閉じる。

## 4. 方法論

本論文は哲学論文であり、経験的方法論ではなく、概念分析・実践記述・理論構築を組み合わせる。

- **二形態区別の方法**: 「創発 as 出来事 (occurrence)」と「創発 as 下向因果 (downward causation)」という emergence 概念の二種に基づいて論を二分し、それぞれに「framework for emergence」と「emerging framework」を対応させる。Table 1 (p.42) は両者を Necessity (External/Internal), Contingency (Relative/Kairotic), Effectiveness (Precision-Openness of guidelines / Collectively-made real-time choices), Artistic Creativity (Extension-Enrichment of vocabulary / Creation of real-time collective norms) の 4 軸で比較する
- **実践者証言の組み入れ**: David Zambrano, Anna Halprin, Simone Forti, Robert Dunn, Frédéric Pouillaude, Laura Aris Álvarez, R. Keith Sawyer などのダンサー・振付師・理論家の一次証言を直接引用する（インタビュー、教則本、エッセイ）
- **哲学的伝統との接続**: Bergson (indefinable resemblance), Jaegwon Kim (emergent property の認識論/形而上学両義), Erin Manning (enabling vs disabling constraints), Lloyd Morgan (emergent evolution 伝統), R. Keith Sawyer (interactional framework) 等を引用して、ダンス即興を哲学的 emergence 論の中で位置付ける
- **situated knowledge の立場**: Donna Haraway を引き、「Haraway が主張するように situated, 身体化された, 体験的知」を認め、データの generalization や Popper 流の falsifiability を求めない (Sec.2, p.35). 論文自身が scientific ではなく conceptual であることを明言する
- **第一人称的実践基盤**: 著者自身が即興ダンサーとして 2024 年 5/11-17 に Laura Aris Álvarez のワークショップで経験した具体的場面 (hand tension の伝播, 足位置の合意形成) を Sec.4.2 で分析事例として提示する (p.39)
- **kairotic contingency 概念**: 事前の必然性 / 相対的偶然性に対し、「即興状況の展開から生じる内的必然性」と「kairos（適切な時機）に開く機会」としての偶然性、という両者が「kairotic contingency」として定義される（Sec.5, p.41）

**判定基準**:
- **強**: 著者が直接その概念・構造を論じており、5段階の定義と明確に対応する

**総合判定**: 本論文は即興ダンスという具体実践を素材に、emergence という概念を通じて「創造性はどのように立ち上がるか」を哲学的に分析する。5 段階モデルの観点からは、Stage 2-5 （波・縁・渦・束）の 4 段階すべてに原文から明示的な対応が抽出できる。特に Stage 4（渦＝ deconstruction → restructuring）と Stage 5（束＝ collective norms / coherent unity）は論文全体の核心構造であり、D28 (舞台・パフォーマンス領域) の中核 evidence として極めて強く使える。Stage 1（場）はやや弱いが、milieu 概念として言及はある。manifest ヒント「Stage 1-4: ダンス即興の創発測定」に対して、本読解では Stage 5 にも強対応し、測定 (measurement) ではなく概念的 framing に力点がある点が異なる。

## 6. 限界・留意事項

- 本論文は「conceptual and explanatory」と自己規定されており、「scientific ではなく predictive でも falsifiable でもない」(Sec.2, p.35) と明言される。evidence として使う際、実証研究として引用することはできず、哲学的概念枠組として扱う必要がある
- 実例は著者自身の第一人称経験 (2024 年 Laura Aris Álvarez ワークショップ) と一部の実践者証言に依拠しており、generalization は原文も否定している (Sec.2, p.35)
- 「emergence」概念は複数分野（複雑系科学、哲学、ダンス研究）で異なる定義を持ち、Randazzo は Jaegwon Kim の metaphysical sense と Lloyd Morgan の emergent evolution 伝統を採用する。D29 (複雑系科学) の同じ「創発」概念 (Munoz D29-S13 等) との接続は可能だが、そのまま等号で結ぶことはできない（前者は現象学的、後者は統計物理学的）
- 本論文の "effectiveness" は通常の手段—目的適合ではなく、「動きの語彙の拡張」と「集合的一貫性の創出」という非功利的効果性である。創造性測定 (cs#123 等) のコンテキストに持ち込む際、この特殊な効果性概念を維持すべきで、performance effectiveness の一般化としては使えない
- 本論文は Randazzo 自身の 2025 年 PhD 論文 "Thinking from Improvised Dance Practices, or Rethinking Subjectivity and the Event in Philosophy" (Université Côte d'Azur) の一部を発展させたものであり、単独論文ではなく一連の思想体系の要約として位置付けられる (References [43], p.45)
- Sawyer の「interactional framework」、Manning の「enabling constraints」、Pouillaude の「allotelic frameworks」等、複数の理論家の概念を Randazzo は選択的に組み合わせているため、各概念の原典での微妙な文脈とは切り離されている場合がある
- 2026 年 3 月公刊のため比較的新しい論文であり、後続の批判的受容はまだない
