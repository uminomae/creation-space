## 2. 要旨

42 頁の包括的レビュー。Stanford 外科学のトップ wound healing 研究室による現代的総説で、皮膚創傷治癒を **5 段階の時空間同期プロセス** として体系化する。冒頭でこう宣言: "Wound healing is one of the most complex processes in the human body. It involves the spatial and temporal synchronization of a variety of cell types with distinct roles in the **phases of hemostasis, inflammation, growth, re-epithelialization, and remodeling**" (p.665, Abstract)。すなわち本論文は **5 段階を著者自身が明示的に宣言する** 数少ない D10 領域の原典。各段階が前段階の終焉と次段階の開始を細胞レベルで条件付ける（platelet → neutrophil/macrophage → fibroblast/endothelial → keratinocyte → myofibroblast/collagen remodeling）動態は 5段階モデルの「波 → 縁 → 渦 → 束」遷移の生物学的具現として読める。

## 3. 主要主張（原文引用付き）

### 主張 1: 創傷治癒は 5 段階の時空間同期プロセス

> "Wound healing is one of the most complex processes in the human body. It involves the spatial and temporal synchronization of a variety of cell types with distinct roles in the **phases of hemostasis, inflammation, growth, re-epithelialization, and remodeling**." (Abstract, p.665)

> "Skin repair requires the intricate synchronization of several different cell types in sequential steps. ... When the skin is wounded, multiple cell types within these three layers need to coordinate at precise stages to bring about healing. These stages of hemostasis, inflammation, angiogenesis, growth, re-epithelialization, and remodeling occur in a temporal sequence but also overlap (167)." (p.665)

著者自身が **5 段階モデル** を明示提示する。重要な点: **(a) 段階は離散的でなく重なる ("temporal sequence but also overlap")、(b) 各段階の主役細胞が異なる、(c) 段階間遷移が impaired wound healing (chronic wounds, fibrosis) の分岐点になる。**

### 主張 2: 段階 I — Hemostasis（止血）3 ステップの自己組織化

> "Hemostasis marks the first stage of wound healing (320) that stops bleeding after vascular damage. It occurs in three steps: vasoconstriction, primary hemostasis, and secondary hemostasis. The critical cell involved in this process is the platelet; the critical matrix component is fibrinogen." (p.666, II.A Hemostasis)

> "When the skin is wounded, the immediate response to stop bleeding is vasoconstriction of the vessel walls. Next, primary hemostasis and secondary hemostasis occur via two concurrent and mechanistically intertwined pathways (129). Primary hemostasis involves platelet aggregation and platelet plug formation that is elicited by exposure of collagen within the subendothelial matrix. Secondary hemostasis refers to the activation of the coagulation cascade where soluble fibrinogen is converted to insoluble strands that make up the fibrin mesh. The platelet plug and the fibrin mesh combine to form the **thrombus**, which stops bleeding, releases complements and growth factors, and provides a provisional scaffold for infiltrating cells necessary for wound healing (320)." (p.666-667)

止血段階は (i) 血管収縮、(ii) 一次止血（platelet plug）、(iii) 二次止血（fibrin mesh）の三層が **mechanistically intertwined** に進行。これ自体が小規模な「波→縁→渦」遷移を構成する。

### 主張 3: 段階 II — Inflammation: DAMPs による recruitment cascade

> "Since wound healing involves several steps, it is important to understand the first signals that activate the cellular response in the wounded tissue. Activation of the transcriptional machinery takes time. Thus the wound first turns on transcription-independent pathways that can readily be activated. These include Ca²⁺ waves, reactive oxygen species (ROS) gradients, and purigenic molecules. Increase of intracellular Ca²⁺ occurs at the wound edges within the first few minutes of injury and propagates to the center of the wound (230). **Damage-associated molecular patterns (DAMPs)**, hydrogen peroxide (H₂O₂), lipid mediators, and chemokines released by injured cells also provide signals for the recruitment of inflammatory cells, especially neutrophils." (p.669, II.B Inflammatory Phase)

> "Neutrophils are usually not observed in the normal skin. They are produced in the bone marrow from promyelocytes and are recruited as 'first responders' from the bone marrow in response to 'find me' signals including hydrogen peroxide, lipid mediators, and chemokines released from regions of injury or infection (393). ... On the day following injury, neutrophils constitute 50% of all cells in the wound (142)." (p.669)

炎症段階は **DAMPs→ neutrophil → macrophage** という **chemoattraction-driven self-organization**。Ca²⁺ wave / ROS gradient / chemokine 勾配が生物学的「波」として伝播し、空間的に細胞動員パターンを規定する。これは 5段階の「波」を最も鮮明に体現する。

### 主張 4: 段階 III — Angiogenesis と Granulation tissue の自己組織化

> "While new blood vessels emerge, resident fibroblasts proliferate and invade the clot to form contractile granulation tissue. Here, some fibroblasts differentiate into myofibroblasts, drawing the wound margins together (263). The dividing fibroblasts deposit ECM and shift the wound microenvironment from the inflammatory to the growth state (445). Re-epithelialization simultaneously occurs and involves the proliferation of both unipotent epidermal stem cells from the basement membrane and de-differentiation of terminally differentiated epidermal cells (90)." (p.666)

> "Pericytes surround endothelial cells in blood vessels and serve critical functions including destabilization and stabilization of microvasculature, regulation of blood flow, and creation of a vascular barrier to bacteria (10, 169, 384, 389). These functions make pericytes indispensible to wound healing." (p.679)

血管新生・線維芽細胞増殖・myofibroblast 分化が **microenvironment shift** （炎症 → 増殖）の境界を引き起こす。この遷移点こそ 5段階の「縁」（境界で起きる質的転換）。

### 主張 5: 段階 IV — Re-epithelialization と幹細胞の plasticity

> "While these epidermal stem cells are mostly unipotent in homeostasis, they become highly plastic in response to injury and can give rise to other cell types to rapidly repair the epidermis during wound healing." (p.666)

> "Stem cells in the hair follicle bulge, for example, do not contribute to the IFE under unwounded conditions. However, after skin injury, cells from the bulge can reprogram and migrate in a linear manner to the center of the wound (FIGURE 6) to reconstitute the IFE where their progeny persist for months following wound healing (194, 243)." (p.682)

> "Following wounding, keratinocytes at the wound edge loosen their adhesions to each other and to the basal lamina to close the defect, forming the migrating **epithelial tongue**." (p.682)

恒常状態では unipotent な幹細胞が、**創傷シグナルに応答して multipotent に reprogram** する。これは 5段階の「渦」（個の立ち上がり・形態転換）を細胞レベルで体現。Hair follicle bulge stem cell が injured IFE に直線的に migrate して新たな表皮を再構築する点は、自己組織化現象の典型例。

### 主張 6: 段階 V — Tissue Maturation and Remodeling: collagen reorganization

> "In most clinical settings, closure of acute and chronic wounds is considered the wound healing end point, but wounds can continue to undergo remodeling and tissue maturation for several months or even years. This last stage of wound healing ultimately determines if scarring will occur or the wound will recur. The remodeling phase consists of regression of the neovasculature, as well as periodic deposition to the ECM and subsequent reconstitution of granulation tissue to scar tissue. Granulation tissue is largely comprised of collagen III, which is partially replaced by the stronger collagen I as remodeling of the wound progresses. This process is a result of concurrent collagen I synthesis and collagen III lysis, which is followed by reorganization of the ECM (167)." (p.685, II.E Tissue Maturation and Remodeling)

> "An imbalance between TIMP and MMP expression can lead to abnormal ECM modification and even chronic wounds (141, 408). As the wound completes the healing process, myofibroblasts undergo mediated apoptosis (182). When cells within the granulation tissue do not undergo apoptosis following wound remodeling, hypertrophic scars tend to form (83)." (p.685)

remodeling 段階は **MMP/TIMP balance** による collagen III → collagen I 再構成と、**myofibroblast apoptosis** による緊張系の解消。balance を失うと chronic wound or hypertrophic scar に分岐する。これは 5段階の「束」（多階層の関係束を整流して安定相に収束）として読める。

### 主張 7: 段階間遷移失敗が病的状態を生む

> "Changes in the microenvironment including alterations in mechanical forces, oxygen levels, chemokines, extracellular matrix and growth factor synthesis directly impact cellular recruitment and activation, leading to impaired states of wound healing." (Abstract, p.665)

> "Neutrophil persistence leads to a prolonged inflammatory state and the emergence of chronic wounds as seen in diabetic foot ulcers, pressure ulcers, and venous leg ulcers (56)." (p.670)

> "Murine studies of hypertrophic scarring support this hypothesis, where following mechanical loading, increased survival of myofibroblasts leads to greater scar tissue. Consequently, myofibroblasts play an important role in the late stages of granulation tissue formation and present a promising target for developing treatments for fibrosis and scarring." (p.681)

**段階間の境界（縁）が突破されなければ pathology に分岐**: 炎症段階の長期化 → chronic wound、myofibroblast apoptosis 失敗 → hypertrophic scar。5段階モデルの境界条件・遷移条件の重要性が病態生理として明示される。

### 主張 8: Fetal vs adult wound healing — 段階構造の発達依存性

> "Wound healing outcomes are linked to the developmental stage of the injured organism. ... fetal skin possesses the remarkable ability to regenerate tissue without scarring (347), heal at an accelerated rate compared with adult wound healing, and result in complete restoration of the dermis, epidermis and epidermal appendages." (p.686)

> "The transition from fetal scarless wound healing into scar formation occurs at a precise gestational stage, at around 18 days gestation in mice (47) and 24 wk gestation in humans." (p.686)

胎児期は **scarless regeneration**、ある gestational stage 以後は **scarring repair** に切り替わる。同じ 5 段階構造でも remodeling 段階の収束様式が異なる。これは 5段階の「束」段階の **発達史的可塑性** を示唆する。

## 4. 方法論

体系的レビュー（systematic narrative review）。450 余りの一次文献を統合し、以下の四層で再構成する:

1. **細胞型別 phenotype 解剖**: 各段階の主役細胞（platelet, neutrophil, macrophage, fibroblast/myofibroblast, endothelial cell + pericyte, keratinocyte, melanocyte）について、起源・surface marker・signaling pathway・段階内役割を網羅
2. **single-cell technologies の活用**: phenotypic / functional heterogeneity の発見（rare stem cell subsets within skin、unipotent → multipotent reprogramming）を強調。これは 2017-2018 年の scRNA-seq 普及を反映
3. **マウス vs ヒト皮膚の差異**: rodent panniculus carnosus による収縮治癒 vs ヒト granulation tissue 形成・re-epithelialization の差異、silicone stent による murine model の修正を明示
4. **病態生理への接続**: 各段階の dysregulation がもたらす chronic wound (DM, vascular disease, hemoglobinopathy) と hypertrophic scarring/keloid を、**段階遷移の失敗** として記述

**D10 視点での方法論的特徴**: 単一細胞技術と複雑系生物学的視座が融合した 2018 年時点の最新統合。古典的 4-phase model（hemostasis, inflammation, proliferation, remodeling）を **5-phase に拡張**（re-epithelialization を独立段階として明示）した点が theoretical contribution。

**判定基準についての注記**:

- **全 5 段階「強」対応**。これは D10 領域で **構造類似最も鮮明な原典の一つ**。著者自身が冒頭 Abstract で「5 phases of hemostasis, inflammation, growth, re-epithelialization, and remodeling」を明示し、各段階が時空間同期する complex process として記述する点が決定的

## 6. 限界・留意事項

- **2018 年論文**: scRNA-seq 普及初期の総説。spatial transcriptomics や organoid wound model など 2020 年以降の技術発展は含まれない
- **マウス偏重**: 多くの mechanistic 知見が murine model 由来。panniculus carnosus による収縮治癒の差異が常時 caveat として記述される
- **臨床応用の限定的成功**: "While several therapies for wound healing are available, these are only moderately effective" (p.665) — 5 段階理解が深まっても臨床的介入は moderate にとどまる現実
- **段階の overlap は強調されるが定量化は限定的**: "occur in a temporal sequence but also overlap" (p.665) と書かれるが、phase overlap を定量する mathematical model は提示されない
- **個体差・年齢差・併存症の扱い**: aging, diabetes, vascular disease 等の影響は記述されるが、systems-level integration は今後の課題として残る
- **D10 領域内の他病態**: 創傷治癒は D10 = clinical medicine の一例。他の 5段階構造類似事例（炎症→消散の Serhan 系、bone healing、神経再生、肝再生、heart failure 段階分類等）への一般化可能性は本論文では扱われない
