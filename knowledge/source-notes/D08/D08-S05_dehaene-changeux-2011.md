## 2. 要旨（読んだ内容に基づく）

Dehaene と Changeux は、意識的処理 (conscious access) の神経基盤を 20 年にわたる経験的・理論的研究から synthesize する。主観的意識経験と観測可能な神経活動の因果関係を確立する試みとして、情報が nonconscious から conscious にどのように遷移するかの "conscious access" 問題に焦点を絞る。Objective な神経指標として 4 つを同定: (1) sensory activity の late amplification, (2) beta-gamma 帯での long-distance cortico-cortical synchronization, (3) 大規模 prefronto-parietal network の "ignition"、(4) 広範囲に分布した P3b 波。これらを Global Neuronal Workspace (GNW) model で説明する: 前頭葉・頭頂葉・帯状回の long-range axon pyramidal neurons が互いに結合し、意識的内容が global に broadcast される network を構成。Subliminal 刺激は early sensory 処理のみ引き起こし、conscious 刺激は "ignition" と呼ばれる self-sustaining reverberating state を発動する。本レビューは: (i) masking, subliminal priming, attentional blink 等の実験パラダイム、(ii) fMRI/ERP/MEG/iEEG/single-unit recording の results、(iii) GNW の computational simulation、(iv) schizophrenia/anesthesia/coma への臨床応用、を統合的にまとめる。

## 3. 主要主張（原文引用付き）

### 主張 1: Conscious access の operational 定義

> "The present review focuses on the well-delimited issue of how an external or internal piece of information goes beyond nonconscious processing and gains access to conscious processing, a transition characterized by the existence of a reportable subjective experience."

"Conscious state" (wakefulness, vigilance) と "conscious access" (reportable content) を明確に区別し、後者に焦点。この定義は実験的に operational かつ定量可能。

### 主張 2: Selective attention と conscious access の解離

> "Selection, also called selective attention, refers to the separation of relevant sensory information from irrelevant information, isolation of an object or spatial location, based on its saliency or relevance to current goals, and amplification of its sensory attributes. Access refers to its conscious 'taking possession of the mind'—the subject of the present review."

William James 1890 の注意定義を精緻化し、attention (selection) と conscious access を解離可能な別プロセスとして定式化。Attention は conscious access の "gateway" だが、両者は等価ではない。

### 主張 3: 4 つの neural signatures of conscious access

> "Four intracranial signatures of conscious perception were identified (Figure 3): (1) late (>300 ms) and distributed event-related potentials contacting sites in prefrontal cortex; (2) large and late (>300 ms) increases in induced power in high-gamma frequencies (50-100 Hz), accompanied by a decrease in lower-frequency power (centered around 10 Hz); (3) increases in long-distance cortico-cortical synchrony in the beta frequency band 13-30 Hz; (4) increases in causal relations across distant electrodes, assessed by Granger causality."

これら 4 signatures は Gaillard et al. 2009 の epileptic patients intracranial recording で同時に observe された収束的知見。

### 主張 4: P3b — 最も robust な conscious access の correlate

> "In ERPs, the most consistent correlate of visibility appears to be a late (~300-500 ms) and broadly distributed positive component called P3 or sometimes P3b."

> "Source modeling related this P3b to a sudden nonlinear ignition, about 300 ms after stimulus presentation, of distributed sources including inferior prefrontal cortex, with a simultaneous reactivation of early visual areas."

P3b は masked/unmasked paradigm で最も systematically conscious vs nonconscious を区別する指標。

### 主張 5: "Ignition" — Global workspace の self-sustaining state

> "The GNW hypothesis (right) proposes that associative perceptual, motor, attention, memory, and value areas interconnect to form a higher-level unified space where information is broadly shared and broadcasted back to lower-level processors."

> "When the ascending vigilance signal is large, several such spontaneous ignitions follow each other in a never-ending 'stream' and can block ignition by incoming external stimuli."

"Ignition" は 2 段階 dynamics: (1) fast feed-forward AMPA 介在波 (<200 ms)、(2) NMDA-mediated reverberating late activation (>300 ms) で late amplification を生成。後者が all-or-none conscious access の閾値的 transition を提供。

### 主張 6: GNW の anatomical 基盤 — Long-distance pyramidal neurons

> "The main anatomical premise of the GNW model is that it consists of 'a distributed set of cortical neurons characterized by their ability to receive from and send back to homologous neurons in other cortical areas horizontal projections through long-range excitatory axons mostly originating from the pyramidal cells of layers II and III'."

> "Layer V PFC pyramidal neurons have as many as 16 times more spines in PFC than in V1, and, as a result, 'the highly spinous cells in prefrontal areas may integrate many more inputs from cells in areas such as V1, TE and 7a'."

Cajal 1899-1904, von Economo 1929 の古典的 neuroanatomical observations と現代 DTI tractography の両方が GNW の long-distance fiber tracts を確認。

### 主張 7: 臨床応用 — Schizophrenia, Anesthesia, Coma

> "Schizophrenia. Friston and Frith (1995) first hypothesized that schizophrenia results from a functional disconnection of long-distance prefrontal cortex projection affecting primarily the strength of N-methyl-D-aspartate receptor (NMDAR)-mediated synaptic transmission."

> "Anesthesia. Anesthetic-induced loss of consciousness usually coincides with the disruption of activity in extensive regions of cerebral cortex, particularly the precuneus, posterior cingulate cortex, cuneus, localized regions of the lateral frontal and parietal cortices."

> "Coma and vegetative state. Monti et al. (2010) observed essentially normal activations of distributed long-distance cortical networks during speech processing and mental imagery tasks, and one patient proved able to voluntarily control them to provide yes/no answers to simple personal questions."

GNW は臨床 3 領域で testable prediction を提供: schizophrenia (NMDA 機能不全で disconnection)、anesthesia (cortical integration の disruption)、coma (residual consciousness の検出)。

### 主張 8: Subliminal 刺激は early 処理のみ誘起、conscious 刺激は late ignition を発動

> "For very brief or low-amplitude stimuli, a feedforward wave was seen in the initial thalamic and cortical stages of the simulation, but it died out without triggering the late global activation, because it was not able to gather sufficient self-sustaining reverberant activation."

Computational simulation で subliminal vs supraliminal stimuli を reproduce。Subliminal は feed-forward sweep のみ、supraliminal は non-linear transition で late amplification を生む。

### 主張 9: Is Conscious Perception Slow and Late? — 2-stage の論争

> "A strong statement of the proposed theoretical synthesis is that early bottom-up sensory events, prior to global ignition (<200-300 ms), contribute solely to nonconscious percept construction and do not systematically distinguish consciously seen from unseen stimuli."

> "Whether it takes 200 ms, 300 ms, or even more, the slow and integrative nature of conscious perception is confirmed behaviorally by observations such as the 'rabbit illusion' and its variants."

GNW の予測: 意識的知覚は early sensory 活動 (<200ms) では区別されず、late ignition (>300ms) で初めて "all-or-none" conscious access が生じる。

### 主張 10: Non-conscious "P3-like" ignition の可能性と GNW 理論の限界

> "Some studies have indeed reported small but significant activations of prefrontal regions and a P3-like wave evoked by infrequent nonconscious stimuli ... However, this wave is usually a novelty P3a response ... rather than the global P3 or 'late positive complex' response evoked by novel stimuli."

GNW の boundary condition を論じる批判的セクション。P3a (attention shift) と P3b (conscious access) の区別が不十分な場合、false positive が生じる。

## 4. 方法論

- **Review 形式**: 新規実験データなし、Dehaene-Changeux グループの 20 年の研究と関連文献の統合
- **Multimodal empirical methods**: fMRI, ERP (EEG), MEG, intracranial recordings (iEEG), single-unit monkey recordings を横断的に集約
- **Computational modeling**: GNW の neural network simulation、spiking neuron model (AMPA/NMDA 受容体を明示的に含む)、thalamocortical loops
- **Clinical 統合**: schizophrenia, anesthesia, coma の 3 臨床領域で GNW prediction を検証
- **比較視座**: 他の理論 (Information Integration Theory by Tononi, Higher-Order Thought by Rosenthal, Global Workspace by Baars) との対話
- **実験パラダイム**: masking, subliminal priming, attentional blink, psychological refractory period, binocular rivalry, no-go tasks

## 5. 5段階との対応候補

| 段階 | 対応候補 | 強度 | 原文引用 |
|------|---------|------|---------|
| 1 場 (Field) | 大脳皮質の広域 neural activation pattern、workspace が展開される anatomical 場 (prefrontal-parietal-cingulate network) | 強 | "a higher-level unified space where information is broadly shared and broadcasted back to lower-level processors" |
| 2 波 (Wave) | Feed-forward sweep (<200 ms)、beta/gamma oscillations、P3b wave の時系列動態 | 強 | "Phase synchrony increased for invisible words in a late time window (300-500 ms) in the beta frequency range (13-30 Hz)" |
| 3 縁 (Relation) | Long-range cortico-cortical projection (L2/3 pyramidal axons)、phase synchrony による area 間結合、NMDA-mediated top-down connection | 強 | "long-distance neurons form brain-scale white matter networks involving prefrontal cortex" |
| 4 渦 (Vortex) | "Ignition" — self-sustaining reverberating state、NMDA feedback loop による閾値的 phase transition | 強 | "the summed postsynaptic potentials evoked by long-distance signaling among these distributed sets of active cells would create slow intracellular currents ... summing up on the cortical surface as negative slow cortical potentials (SCPs) over regions coding for the conscious stimulus" |
| 5 束 (Bundle) | Conscious access — 感覚・運動・注意・記憶・価値が一体化した "unified space" としての意識統合 | 強 | "associative perceptual, motor, attention, memory, and value areas interconnect to form a higher-level unified space" |

**判定基準**:
- Stage 1 (強): Workspace を anatomical/functional field として明示的に空間化
- Stage 2 (強): Temporal dynamics (oscillations, ERP components) を中心的 analysis 対象とする
- Stage 3 (強): Long-range connections と synchrony を結合機構として定式化
- Stage 4 (強): "Ignition" は self-sustaining dynamic steady-state の典型。意識的 access の nonlinear phase transition として「渦」概念と強く対応
- Stage 5 (強): 異なる modality・機能が一体化した workspace は「束ね」の明示的パラダイム

## 6. 限界・留意事項

- **Conscious access に焦点絞り**: Higher-order consciousness (self-awareness, metacognition, extended consciousness) は本レビューの射程外
- **Prefrontal centrism の批判**: GNW は prefrontal cortex の central role を仮定するが、PFC lesion でも意識が保持されるケース (Pollen 1999) の存在
- **Intracranial data の限定**: iEEG は epileptic patients のみから、cortical coverage は limited
- **Animal consciousness**: 本論文では human に焦点、non-human 動物への拡張は future direction
- **Qualia 問題**: 意識の "hard problem" (subjective experience の物理的説明) は operational には扱わず、conscious access の neural correlates に議論を限定
- **Competing theories**: IIT (Tononi), recurrent theory (Lamme), higher-order theories 等との empirical adjudication は不完全
- **Simulation の scalability**: GNW simulation は 4 areas のみ、human cortex 全体を含まない
- **NMDA antagonist 実験**: ketamine/PCP で conscious access が保持される場合があり、simple NMDA-GNW association の限界
