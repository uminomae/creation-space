# 5-Stage Model Reference

The 5-stage model describes structural patterns of creation across domains. It is NOT a claim of mathematical isomorphism -- it identifies recurring qualitative patterns.

## Stages

### 1. 場 (Ba / Field)

- **Color**: `#8B8682`
- **Description**: Undifferentiated initial state. No direction or structure yet. The raw ground from which creative processes begin.

### 2. 波 (Nami / Wave)

- **Color**: `#5B8DB8`
- **Description**: Multiple directions diverge and compete. This is the exploration phase where possibilities expand and collide without resolution.

### 3. 縁 (En / Edge)

- **Color**: `#D4A857`
- **Description**: Opposing elements coexist in tension without converging. The boundary where relationships form between previously separate elements. This is the critical transition zone.

### 4. 渦 (Uzu / Vortex)

- **Color**: `#C45B4D`
- **Description**: New order spontaneously emerges from tension. An irreversible reorganization occurs -- the system cannot return to its prior state.

### 5. 束 (Taba / Bundle)

- **Color**: `#5B8B6A`
- **Description**: Form stabilizes as reusable structure. The emergent pattern becomes a durable artifact that can be referenced, shared, or built upon.

## Key Finding

Across 30 domains analyzed, **縁 (Edge)** appears in 28/30 domains as the most prominent stage. This suggests that the coexistence of tension -- the boundary condition where opposing elements meet without premature resolution -- is the most universally recognizable structural pattern in creative processes.

## Usage in Infographics

- Each stage is represented by its designated color
- Judgment strength (strong/partial/conditional/none) maps to opacity levels (see design-system.md)
- Stage labels use both Japanese and English: e.g., "場 Field", "波 Wave"
